# Giant Air Spear

A new **Air basic arcana** (#85, part of the giant weapon set). It's a huge spear swung slowly: two long stabs along your aim, then a throw. The thrown spear flies out, piercing everything in its path, then comes back to your hand and hits again on the way back.

| Hit | Default damage | Behaviour |
|---|---:|---|
| 1-2: stab | 12 | Wind burst on every enemy within 3.4 u along the aim (0.9 u wide) |
| 3: throw | 16 out, 16 back | Flies 6.5 u at 16 u/s, returns at 20 u/s, hits each enemy once each way |
| Enhanced gust | 10 | Enhanced: a 1.6 u gust where the spear turns |

Enhanced also gives +25% stab reach and throw range. It's slow, as a giant weapon should be: a 0.24 s wind-up on each stab and 0.32 s on the throw, with a late cancel. Gear activation speed shortens all of it.

It uses WoL Art's spear (#44) drawn at giant scale. All numbers are in `BepInEx\config\mdbailey94.wol.giantspear.cfg`. Requires LegendAPI.
