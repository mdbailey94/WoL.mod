# Buckler

A new **Earth basic arcana** (#87, part of the giant weapon set). It's a small stone shield worn for punching: three fast, short punches with no charge. While the buckler is out it breaks any enemy shot it meets, using the same checks as Stone Charge's shield; a boomerang is sent back instead.

| Hit | Default damage | Behaviour |
|---|---:|---|
| 1-2: punch | 7 | Earth burst 1.0 u in front, 0.75 u radius |
| 3: last punch | 11 | Earth burst 1.0 u in front, 1.0 u radius, bigger knockback |
| Enhanced shrapnel | 5 | Enhanced: each shot the buckler breaks bursts into rocks (0.8 u) |

It's fast: 0.08 s wind-up and an early cancel, with a step into each punch. The buckler stays out for 0.3 s per punch and breaks shots within 0.8 u of it.

Art: WoL Art's buckler, 4-frame punch and icon ('Giant weapons (2026-11-03)'). All numbers are in `BepInEx\config\mdbailey94.wol.buckler.cfg`. Requires LegendAPI.
